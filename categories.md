# Categories

Each post gets one **primary** label and up to two **secondary** labels. Use the quoted tweet / link title as context: a post saying "Exactly" that quotes an immigration story is `immigration_crime`.

| id | Category | Covers | Roll-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| us_politics | US politics & elections | Parties, candidates, voting, Congress, courts, partisan commentary ("the radical left"), Trump/Harris/Biden | Politics |
| government | Government spending & DOGE | Deficit, waste, DOGE, USAID, bureaucracy, regulation, taxes, Social Security | Politics |
| intl_politics | International politics | UK, EU, Germany, Brazil, Ukraine/Russia, Venezuela, China, etc. | Politics |
| immigration_crime | Immigration & crime | Illegal immigration, borders, grooming gangs, crime stories, policing | Politics |
| free_speech | Free speech & censorship | Censorship, deplatforming, government pressure on platforms, free-speech rights | Rights & media |
| media | Legacy-media criticism | Attacks on / commentary about news outlets and journalists, "you are the media" | Rights & media |
| culture_society | Culture & society | Woke / DEI / gender, religion, Western civilization, birth rates & population, education | Rights & media |
| tesla | Tesla | Cars, FSD, Autopilot, Robotaxi, Optimus, Tesla Energy, factories, shareholders, TSLA | Companies |
| spacex | SpaceX & Starlink | Launches, Starship, Falcon, Dragon, Starlink, Mars, space exploration | Companies |
| x_platform | X / Twitter | Platform features, Community Notes, advertisers, X Payments, the acquisition | Companies |
| ai | AI, xAI & Grok | Grok, xAI, AI capabilities and safety, OpenAI disputes | Companies |
| other_ventures | Other ventures | Neuralink, The Boring Company, SolarCity, PayPal/Zip2 history | Companies |
| science_tech | Science & engineering | Physics, engineering, technology, energy, climate science not tied to one of his companies | Science & tech |
| economy | Economy & markets | Inflation, interest rates, markets, business in general (not his companies) | Economy |

| news_events | News & events (non-political) | Disasters, tragedies, condolences, sports, celebrities, notable events | News |
| humor | Humor & memes | Jokes, memes, puns, shitposts with no other clear subject | Personal & humor |
| personal | Personal & recommendations | Family, daily life, books, games, music, films, philosophy | Personal & humor |
| low_content | Unclassifiable | Bare reactions (Wow, !!, 🤣) or link-only posts where even the context gives no topic | — |

**Primary-label rule:** pick the topic a reader would say the post is *about*. Company promotion beats general topic (a Starlink disaster-relief post is `spacex`, not `news_events`); political framing beats the vehicle ("Tesla is being targeted by the radical left" → `us_politics` primary, `tesla` secondary).
